Dig privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Dig is an iPhone and iPad app made by Lucian Cioranu. Contact: wedigitapp@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Everything you save in Dig (links, posts, notes, photos, documents and chats) is stored on your iPhone or iPad. Dig has no account and no servers, and its maker receives none of it. If you turn on Backup, a copy is saved to your own iCloud Drive or to a folder you choose.

Your AI provider

To summarize and organize what you share, Dig sends its text, images and your note straight from your device to the AI you choose in Settings. That is Apple (Apple Intelligence, on the device or on Private Cloud Compute), OpenAI (your ChatGPT plan or an API key), Anthropic (Claude) or Google (Gemini). Their privacy policies apply to what they receive. Sign-ins and keys are kept in your device's Keychain. Choose Apple Intelligence on the device to keep everything on your iPhone.

Reading links

Dig opens the public web page of each link you share, from your device, to read it. It also re-reads the shop pages of products you hearted, to check their prices. Those sites see a normal visit from your device.

Your area

If you allow it, Dig uses your approximate area (city and country, never your exact position) so suggested shops and events start near you. Only the place name is sent, to your AI provider.

Asking for Dig AI

If you tap "I'd use this" in Settings › AI › Dig AI, Dig counts it once in its iCloud database at Apple: your country, which AI you use and the app version. It holds no name, no email and nothing you saved.

Ads and tracking

Dig has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Children

Dig is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

Everything Dig stores is on your device. Delete a save in the app, or delete the app to remove it all. A backup you made stays in your iCloud Drive or folder until you delete it there.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
