Help with Dig for iPhone and iPad

Write to wedigitapp@gmail.com. Say what you did, what you expected and what happened, and which iPhone or iPad you use.

In the app

Settings › Help answers the common questions: how to save, what a save shows, Finds and price alerts, boards, chat, the AI choices and where your saves are kept. Settings is the gear button at the top of Library.

Common questions

Dig isn't in the Share list. In the Share sheet, scroll the row of apps to the end, tap More and turn Dig on.

A save has no summary. No AI is set up yet. Open Settings › AI and choose Apple Intelligence, your ChatGPT plan or your own key.

Only the link was saved. Some apps don't let other apps read a post. Share a screenshot or a screen recording of it to Dig.

A price wasn't checked. Some shops change their pages, so a price can't always be read. Pull down in Finds to check again.

Moving to a new iPhone. Turn on Settings › Backup on the old phone. On the new one, install Dig and it offers to restore.
